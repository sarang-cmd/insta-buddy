A calmer path through a messy folder.

The app reads what is already in your files, scores each frame locally and keeps the decisions visible. You can change any keeper, suggestion or caption before export.

  1. 01

    Group the roll

    Capture time, file time and visual similarity split the trip into sessions and moments. Bursts and near-duplicates land together, with the source of each timestamp shown in the app.

  2. 02

    Choose a keeper

    Sharpness, exposure and colour scores pick one frame from each moment. The rest are labelled retake or alt, with a readable reason, and you can promote a different frame at any time.

  3. 03

    Finish the set

    Reversible touch-ups, local caption drafts and a feed grid come together in one ZIP. Desktop adds native save dialogs and encrypted key storage for optional AI providers.

Your photos do not need a server.

Decoding, analysis, grouping, retouching and export run in the browser or desktop app. AI is a separate, off-by-default choice, and sending photos to a cloud provider needs its own opt-in.

Questions, answered.

Details that matter before you hand over a folder of photos.

Do I need an AI provider?

No. All 12 providers are optional and off by default. The on-device engine handles grouping, keeper selection, touch-ups, captions and export.

Where do my photos go?

Decoding, scoring, edits and export happen in the browser or desktop app. Photos are not uploaded unless you enable an AI feature and allow that feature to send images to a provider you chose.

What is the demo trip?

The app paints procedural holiday photos locally so you can try the whole workflow without adding files. They are simulated images, not real customer photos.

What does the desktop app add?

Native file and save dialogs, AI keys stored encrypted in the OS keychain, and AI requests proxied from the main process so those keys never enter the page.

What can I export?

A ZIP with edited JPEGs at your chosen long edge, caption text and JSON files, and a feed grid image.

What are the limits?

People detection is a heuristic, thin wires over busy backgrounds can be missed, extend uses mirrored edge synthesis, and local captions are template-generated. Every result stays editable.
